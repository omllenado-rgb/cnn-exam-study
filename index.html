📘 How to Use the CNN Study App

1. Getting started

Open the app on your phone or computer, sign in with your name and a password, and your progress saves automatically so you can pick up on any device.

2. Quiz

Practice questions. Pick a topic or All, then Start Quiz. Every question shows an explanation, and questions shuffle each time. Use the Weak-Topic Drill to auto-build 15 questions from your lowest-accuracy topics.

3. Lessons & Strategies

Lessons teach key concepts in plain language. Strategies are quick study tips you can skim between shifts.

4. Practice Test

Three full 150-question simulations weighted to the official CNN blueprint, with a 3-hour timer. Turn on exam-day mode to practice with no feedback until the end.

5. Flashcards

Spaced-repetition cards. Cards you miss come back sooner so you master them faster.

6. Trackers

See your accuracy, study days, streak, and progress toward the 80-hour goal. Use AI Evaluate for a personalized read on your weak topics, and log study time you did outside the app.

7. How to log hours

On the Trackers tab, scroll to the Log Study Hours card, enter the hours (decimals allowed, e.g. 1.5 = 1 hr 30 min), pick a category, and tap Add.
